### Initial Release
- Customer management: keep track of customers, contacts, and every follow-up in one place.
- Opportunity tracking: follow each deal's stage, value, and progress so nothing slips away.
- Dashboard: see key numbers and follow-up reminders at a glance.
- Fast to navigate: search, filter, and paginate; switching views keeps your place.
- Change history: edits to customers or opportunities are recorded automatically for easy review.
- Data backup: admins can back up, restore, download, and delete data in one click to keep it safe.
- Works everywhere: smooth on both phone and desktop.
